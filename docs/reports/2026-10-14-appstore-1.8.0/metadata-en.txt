STTBar turns your voice into finished text, in any app on your Mac.

Press a hotkey and speak. Press it again and STTBar transcribes the recording, on your Mac or on a server of your choice. The text appears where your cursor is: in an email, a chat, your editor or the prompt field of an AI assistant.

What STTBar does:
• Three hotkeys: cleaned-up text, raw transcript and English output (speak German, paste English).
• On-device speech recognition with Parakeet or WhisperKit. After the model download, recognition works offline. Local models are preloaded when the app starts.
• Or a Whisper server: on a computer in your network or on the internet, with an API key if it needs one.
• Optional cleanup with a language model of your choice: locally, for example in LM Studio or Ollama, or hosted through OpenRouter and other OpenAI-compatible services. It removes filler words and gives the text a clear structure. Cleanup mode can fall back to the raw transcript if enabled. English output requires a working language model endpoint.
• API keys stay in your Mac's keychain and only go to the server they belong to.
• A small overlay with a live waveform shows whether STTBar is recording, transcribing or cleaning up.
• Word replacements for names and jargon, so they are always spelled right.
• History with privacy controls, and a sensitive mode that never stores a dictation.
• German or English interface.

No account, no subscription, no ads. Recordings and texts leave your Mac only if you add a server or service yourself.

STTBar needs access to the microphone and to Accessibility. Accessibility is required to insert the text into the active app.

Requires macOS 14 and a Mac with Apple silicon. On-device recognition downloads model files once. The first download and preparation can take 1 to 5 minutes.
